WHO WE ARE

Technology should be accessible to everyone.

CaraTech Studios was created in Caraga Region based in Surigao City with a simple idea: technology should help people solve real problems without requiring an expensive budget.

We focus on developing websites, applications, robotics prototypes, and technical solutions that support businesses, students, schools, and local communities.

Our goal is to combine creativity, engineering, and innovation to turn ideas into useful technology.
